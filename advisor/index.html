Open Doors: Russian Scholarship Project

Global Universities Association, with the Ministry of Science and Higher Education

Eligible100
  • •Your age fits the master's track band of 20 to 33.
  • •Some evidenced achievements will help, but the portfolio is where this is won or lost.
  • •You may submit the portfolio in English, and most participating universities teach some programmes in English.
  • •Free to enter, and judged on merit rather than a country allocation.

Russian Government Scholarship (state quota)

Rossotrudnichestvo and the Ministry of Science and Higher Education

Eligible85
  • •A funded preparatory year of Russian is normally included, which suits you.
  • •Most quota places lead to Russian-taught programmes, so check English availability carefully.
  • •The largest single source of funded places, at roughly 15,000 a year worldwide.

Verify before you rely on this

This matcher applies published eligibility rules and the cost data in this app. It cannot see your grades, your references or your competition, and it does not predict whether you will win. Use it to narrow a shortlist, then verify every rule on the official site.

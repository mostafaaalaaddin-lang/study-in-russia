Funded routes

Every way to study in Russia without paying tuition

Six routes, what each one actually covers, and the part of the cost each one quietly leaves with you. Two of them are national competitions; the rest are worth knowing about because most applicants never hear of them.

Open Doors: Russian Scholarship Project

Main route

Global Universities Association, with the Ministry of Science and Higher Education

Very high competition

An open academic competition rather than an application queue. You build a portfolio, sit a subject exam or an interview, and winners receive a tuition-free place inside the state quota at one of 24 participating universities.

Levels

Bachelor, Master, PhD, Postdoctoral

Tuition

Full

Monthly stipend

1,500 ₽ to 6,500 ₽

Window

Registration 20 August - 1 November 2026; portfolio closes 13 November 2026

✓ Stipend✓ Dormitory place— Dormitory fee paid— Flights— Insurance— Living costs
Official source↗Free. There is no participation fee at any stage.

Who can apply

  • •Foreign citizens, stateless persons and compatriots living abroad
  • •Bachelor's track: age 16-23, secondary school certificate or vocational diploma, no existing degree
  • •Master's track: age 20-33, a Bachelor or Specialist degree, no existing Master's degree
  • •Doctoral track: age 22-35, a Master or Specialist degree, no completed PhD
  • •Postdoctoral track: age 24-39, holding a PhD, Candidate of Sciences or Doctor of Sciences
  • •Students graduating in the current academic year may apply

Language

The competition runs in Russian or English. You may submit your portfolio in either language, and English-taught programmes exist at most participating universities.

Not covered

  • ✗International flights
  • ✗Visa fees and document legalisation
  • ✗Medical insurance
  • ✗Dormitory fees
  • ✗Food, transport and daily living costs

Steps

  1. 1Register a participant account on od.globaluni.ru between 20 August and 1 November 2026
  2. 2Choose your subject area, and check which of the 24 universities accept that area
  3. 3Take the entry test inside your account
  4. 4Write a separate motivation letter for every subject area you enter
  5. 5Record your achievements in the portfolio fields and upload a scan evidencing each one
  6. 6Bachelor's and Master's candidates sit a proctored 180-minute online exam before 21 December 2026
  7. 7Doctoral and postdoctoral candidates book a supervisor interview between 22 December 2026 and 26 February 2027
  8. 8If you win, complete the standard university admission and student visa process

Watch out

  • !Registration closes on 1 November, almost two weeks before the portfolio deadline. Missing that date ends your cycle.
  • !Portfolio results cannot be appealed.
  • !An achievement with no uploaded document behind it is the single most common reason a strong profile scores badly.
  • !Runners-up only receive a place if quota places are left over.

Russian Government Scholarship (state quota)

Main route

Rossotrudnichestvo and the Ministry of Science and Higher Education

High competition

The main state route, with roughly 15,000 places distributed annually across more than 180 countries. Your local Russian embassy or Rossotrudnichestvo office runs the first round of selection.

Levels

Preparatory year, Bachelor, Master, PhD

Tuition

Full

Monthly stipend

1,500 ₽ to 6,500 ₽

Window

Usually opens in November and closes in February, but the exact dates are set country by country

✓ Stipend✓ Dormitory place— Dormitory fee paid— Flights— Insurance— Living costs
Official source↗Free through the official portal and your local Russian mission.

Preparatory faculty year (Russian language foundation)

Russian universities, funded inside the state quota

Moderate competition

A funded year of intensive Russian before your degree starts. If your programme is taught in Russian and you do not speak it yet, this year is normally part of the package rather than an extra cost.

Levels

Preparatory year

Tuition

Full

Monthly stipend

1,500 ₽ to 2,500 ₽

Window

Granted together with a quota place, so it follows the quota timetable

✓ Stipend✓ Dormitory place— Dormitory fee paid— Flights— Insurance— Living costs
Official source↗No separate application when it comes as part of a quota award.

University scholarships and tuition discounts

Individual Russian universities

Moderate competition

Discounts and merit awards a university funds from its own budget. They rarely cover everything, but they stack with your own funds and are far less competitive than the national programmes.

Levels

Bachelor, Master, PhD

Tuition

Partial

Monthly stipend

Varies or none

Window

Tied to each university admission cycle, commonly spring to summer

— Stipend✓ Dormitory place— Dormitory fee paid— Flights— Insurance— Living costs
Official source↗Usually free, though some universities charge a document processing fee.

Academic excellence stipends for enrolled students

Russian state and university funds

Moderate competition

Raised stipends paid to students who perform well after enrolment. Not a route into Russia, but a reason to take your first semester seriously.

Levels

Bachelor, Master, PhD

Tuition

None

Monthly stipend

Varies or none

Window

Assessed each semester once you are enrolled

Bilateral intergovernmental agreements

Your national ministry of higher education, with Russian counterparts

High competition

Some countries negotiate their own allocation of places with Russia and run the selection themselves. Applicants routinely miss these because they only watch the Russian portals.

Levels

Bachelor, Master, PhD

Tuition

Full

Monthly stipend

Varies or none

Window

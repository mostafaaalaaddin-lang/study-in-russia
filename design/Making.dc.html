Study in Russia
How the project was built

Why this project exists

I built the tool I needed.

A planning tool for applicants to Russian universities, built on the official rules.

The problem

Every guide I found stopped at the phrase fully funded. None answered the expensive questions: what the award actually pays for, what it quietly leaves with you, and what a month in the city you end up in really costs.

So I read the official rules myself, turned the cost of living in eight cities into numbers anyone can compare, and built the three tools I wanted while applying.

What it does

Documents six funded routes with their official rules, dates and coverage.

Costs a month of student life in eight Russian cities, down to the rouble.

Matches a profile to a route, answers questions with sources, reviews a letter.
